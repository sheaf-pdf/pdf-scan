## TIFF: the `tiff` crate's coverage, maintenance and fax gaps; other Rust TIFF crates; libtiff

### Takeaway
`tiff` 0.11.3 (2026-02-10, MIT, image-rs) is still the latest release. It reads the strip and tile basics well: none, LZW, Deflate, PackBits, new-style JPEG, Fax4, ZSTD and WebP; planar; predictors; BigTIFF. It writes multi-page TIFF and BigTIFF with none, LZW, Deflate or PackBits. It writes nothing 1-bit or CCITT, and doesn't apply Orientation. Its G4 reader takes widths and heights only up to 65,535. Fax3 (1D only, with FillOrder) was merged on `main` on 2026-03-12 and is still unreleased nine months on. A Modified Huffman PR has sat unreviewed since 2026-09. **Verdict: use it directly for IFD parsing, uncompressed/LZW/Deflate/PackBits/JPEG strips and LZW writing. Route every CCITT strip (compressions 2, 3 and 4) to hayro-ccitt in `scan-image`. Write G4 TIFF directories ourselves, as sheaf already does. Contribute upstream only where it's cheap: Fax3 2D/T4Options, u32 G4 dimensions, a release.**

### Inferences
- **[corrects entry 16]** The gap isn't only "G3 and MH refused". The release refuses G3 and MH. `main` reads G3 1D only and would read 2D-coded G3 wrong, since it never looks at T4Options. Both refuse G4 images over 65,535 px wide. A 70,000-px-wide G4 TIFF that BUG-1's fix lets `scan-image` write could not be read back through `tiff`'s Fax4 path. So `scan-image` should take raw strip bytes from `tiff`'s IFD (StripOffsets/StripByteCounts) and decode compressions 2, 3 and 4 through hayro-ccitt, the same path `pdf-images` uses. That fixes BUG-2 for every K and also the >65,535 case, and it needs no release from image-rs.
- BUG-3: two-sample gray is fixable in `scan-image` itself, by mapping `Multiband { num_samples: 2 }` under BlackIsZero/WhiteIsZero to gray plus alpha (or gray plus a dropped extra sample). It needs nothing upstream. The planar case is sheaf's own refusal (entry 16 had this right).
- Writing: `tiff` 0.11.3 can't write a 1-bit or CCITT image through its `ImageEncoder`, so the G4 TIFF writer stays our own directory writer, through `DirectoryEncoder` with pre-compressed strips. LZW and Deflate RGB/gray multi-page output can use `tiff` directly.
- image-rs's stance on fax compressions: it accepts decoders (Fax4 in 0.10.3, Fax3 1D merged), prefers codec fixes in `fax`, and is slow to review and release. That makes "contribute upstream" a slow path that shouldn't block S1.
- libtiff is a permissive reference and port source for G3/MH encoding (fax3.c) if we ever need to write G3. Keep the notices. Avoid the `tif_lzw.c` advertising clause (weezl covers LZW).

## CCITT G3/G4: `fax`, `hayro-ccitt`, other decoders and any G3 encoder

### Takeaway
**Decode: hayro-ccitt 0.5.0 (2026-11-02), use directly.** It covers G4, G3 1D, G3 2D, EOLs (auto-detected), byte-aligned rows (so MH), end of block, damaged G3 lines and a strict mode; it is `forbid(unsafe)`, `no_std` and dependency-free; MSRV 1.92; there's one maintainer. **G4 encode: `fax`, use it and contribute upstream.** But 0.3.0 has its own wide-image bug (an i16 vertical-mode overflow above 32,767 px), fixed on master on 2026-11-05 and not yet released, so pin 0.3.1 or a git rev before taking BUG-1's 70,000-px test. **G3/MH encode: nothing mature in Rust. Build our own if fixtures or output need it (port from libtiff's fax3, permissive), or generate fixtures with libtiff out of process.**

### Inferences
- BUG-1's planned check ("a black-and-white page 70,000 × 100 px round-tripping") runs with overflow checks on, so on fax 0.3.0 it would hit the issue #30 panic whenever a vertical-mode delta straddles 32,767. ## JPEG: decoders, encoders, lossless transforms, EXIF orientation

### Takeaway
**Decode: zune-jpeg 0.5.15, use directly** (and contribute PDF quirks upstream). It decodes 8-bit baseline, extended and progressive Huffman JPEGs, with CMYK and Adobe APP14. 12-bit DCT, lossless and arithmetic JPEGs fail: 0.5.16-rc3 adds an `arith` feature and parses 12-bit headers, but still decodes 8-bit pixels only. **Encode: jpeg-encoder 0.7.1, use directly.** It does CMYK, custom quantization tables and progressive, and image's `main` is moving to it. mozjpeg-rs 0.9.2 (BSD-3, `forbid(unsafe)`) is the pure-Rust option if size matters. **Lossless transforms (jpegtran): only libjpeg-turbo-rs 0.9.0 does them in pure Rust. It's young, carries ~326 `unsafe`, and shows signs of agent-loop development, so study only. Put the EXIF turn in `/Rotate` (PDF) and keep the JPEG bytes. Build our own coefficient-domain rotate later if files need it. EXIF orientation: build our own tiny APP1 IFD0 reader (sheaf has one), with kamadak-exif 0.6.2 as the fallback.**

- Lossless transforms aren't needed for PDF output (`/Rotate` carries the EXIF turn, as `pdf-scan` plans). They matter only for JPEG *file* output of a turned page. If that's wanted, a coefficient-domain rotate is roughly baseline/progressive Huffman decode to blocks, transpose or flip with sign flips, then Huffman re-encode. libjpeg-turbo's `transupp.c` (IJG licence, permissive with notice) is the port reference and libjpeg-turbo-rs a study source. libjpeg-turbo-rs's own weight (72k lines, unsafe in core paths) and the `ralph/` exclusion (the name of an autonomous coding-loop technique) argue against taking it as a dependency yet.

## JBIG2: decoders, and whether a lossless generic-region-only Rust encoder is realistic

### Takeaway
**Decode: hayro-jbig2 0.4.0 (2026-11-02), use directly and contribute upstream.** It is still marked "currently under development", but 0.4.0 added a strict mode, multi-page files, shared `Globals`, the 12-AT-pixel extended template, and many fixes for malformed and refinement cases. Every other pure-Rust decoder is either licence-tainted (justbig2 is ported from AGPL jbig2dec; jbig2enc-rust has GPL history) or too new (Microsoft's jbig2-decoder 0.1, three days old). **Encode: nothing usable is permissive and mature. A lossless generic-region-only encoder is realistic (≈500–800 lines: the MQ coder, the template-0 context loop and segment headers). It is portable from agl/jbig2enc (Apache-2.0) or SerenityOS's JBIG2Writer (BSD-2), and verifiable against hayro-jbig2 and PDFium. So build our own, but only if a corpus measurement shows it beats G4 enough to matter. Otherwise the plan's "never JBIG2 encoded" stands.**

### Inferences
- A lossless generic-region encoder is realistic and small. It needs the MQ encoder (the inverse of the decoder hayro-jbig2 already has), the template-0 context loop with the default AT pixels (TPGDON is optional), and the segment headers: page information, immediate lossless generic region, end of page, plus the embedded-stream form for PDF (no file header, no `/JBIG2Globals`). Port it from agl/jbig2enc (Apache-2.0, keep the NOTICE) or SerenityOS (BSD-2). Round-trip it through hayro-jbig2 and PDFium. This avoids the Xerox failure mode completely: there is no symbol matching.
- Pixel-exact symbol coding (reuse only on bitmap equality, as jbig2enc-rust's `text_lossless()`) is also safe from digit swaps, but it is far more code. Leave it out.
- Whether to encode JBIG2 at all is a size question nobody has answered for scans. Measure lossless generic JBIG2 against G4 on the corpus's 150 CCITT and 127 JBIG2 scans first. JBIG2Decode is a PDF 1.4 filter, so PDF/A (unlike with JPX in PDF/A-1) shouldn't be the blocker. This is from memory, not checked this session.
- **License rule outcomes:** justbig2 is an AGPL-derived port published as MIT/Apache, so it can be neither used nor ported. jbig2enc-rust's own NOTICE leaves its provenance unresolved, so don't depend on it or port from it. jbig2dec is study only (AGPL).
- hayro-jbig2's "under development" label is now cosmetic more than real: it has 5 releases, multi-page and globals support, a strict mode and fuzzing. Its colour-extension rejection is correct for PDF (JBIG2 in PDF is bilevel).

## PNG and Deflate (png, zune-png, image-png, zlib-rs, miniz_oxide), and JPEG XL briefly

### Takeaway
**PNG: `png` 0.18.1, use directly.** It forbids unsafe, is image-rs's crate and runs on OSS-Fuzz. zune-png is faster but expands sub-8-bit images to 8 bits, which is wrong for 1-bit scans. **Deflate for PDF streams: miniz_oxide (forbids unsafe) or fdeflate; zlib-rs is faster but heavy in unsafe (a zlib-ng port).** Pick by measured speed against the pure-Rust rule; entry 65's "default to zlib-rs" trades safety for speed. **JPEG XL: watch only.** `jxl` (jxl-rs, BSD-3, the decoder in Chrome and Firefox) has joined jxl-oxide; PDF's JPEG XL text is still unconfirmed.

## The `image` crate's role, and image-rs's stance on Fax3/MH in `tiff`

### Takeaway
`image` 0.25.10 (2026-03-10, MSRV 1.88) delegates JPEG decoding to zune-jpeg, PNG to png, TIFF to tiff, GIF to gif and WebP to image-webp. It keeps its own BMP and JPEG encoder, and its `main` is moving JPEG encoding to jpeg-encoder. Its own changelog says "Many decoders will panic on malicious input". image-rs accepts fax decoding in `tiff` (Fax4 released; Fax3 1D merged, unreleased) but leaves MH unreviewed. **Verdict: use `image` directly for BMP, GIF and WebP input and as the pixel-buffer type, with default features off. Call `tiff`, `png`, zune-jpeg and hayro-ccitt directly where the plan needs control: CCITT, 1-bit, resolution, orientation.**

## Test corpora, conformance suites and fuzzing for these formats

### Takeaway

## Holes in the ecosystem

### Takeaway
What no adequate permissive, pure-Rust and mature implementation covers as of 2026-11-08: (1) G3 (1D/2D) and MH **encoding**; (2) a released `tiff` that reads G3 2D/MH or G4 wider than 65,535 px; (3) lossless JPEG transforms (rotate, flip, crop) in safe Rust; (4) 12-bit DCT and (in a stable release) arithmetic-coded JPEG decoding in a safe crate; (5) a lossless JBIG2 encoder with clean provenance; (6) a mature JPEG 2000 encoder; (7) independent conformance results for hayro-ccitt, hayro-jbig2 and hayro-jpeg2000, none of which is on OSS-Fuzz; and (8) a released `fax` without the >32,767-px G4 encoder panic.

### Cited Findings
- G3/MH encoding: fax encodes G4 only ([fax 0.3.0 README](https://docs.rs/crate/fax/0.3.0/source/README.md)); SerenityOS encodes G4 only ([CCITTEncoder.h](https://github.com/SerenityOS/serenity/blob/master/Userland/Libraries/LibGfx/ImageFormats/CCITTEncoder.h)); only oxideav-tiff 0.0.9 (859 downloads) and an unreviewed tiff PR #414 (MH) have encoders ([oxideav-tiff ccitt.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/oxideav-tiff/0.0.9/source/src/ccitt.rs); [PR #414](https://github.com/image-rs/image-tiff/pull/414))
- tiff release gaps: [tiff 0.11.3 image.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/tiff/0.11.3/source/src/decoder/image.rs); [stream.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/tiff/0.11.3/source/src/decoder/stream.rs)
- Lossless transforms: only libjpeg-turbo-rs (unsafe-heavy, "no memory-safety guarantee") — [README](https://docs.rs/crate/libjpeg-turbo-rs/0.9.0/source/README.md); the others are AGPL or C bindings ([zenjpeg](https://crates.io/crates/zenjpeg), [turbojpeg](https://crates.io/crates/turbojpeg))

### Inferences
- Holes 1, 2 and 8 hit S1's BUG-1 and BUG-2 directly. The cheapest path is the one recommended above: hayro-ccitt for all CCITT decoding, `fax` after #31 for G4, and libtiff out of process for G3/MH fixtures.
- Holes 3 to 6 are later-milestone concerns (M11, M13, M14). Measure on the corpus before building anything.

### Gaps
- None beyond those listed in the sections above.
